
Autonomous Self-Healing Health, Nutrition & Rehab Engine

An Open-Core, multi-agent biometric immune system that analyzes meal photos, optimizes training, and prevents rehab injuries—continuously adapting to your real-time biology.

Why This Exists

Static fitness apps and generic AI chatbots fail because human biology isn't static. If you eat off-plan, sleep poorly, or feel a twinge in your knee during squats, a static plan breaks.
Instead of acting like a passive chatbot, this engine operates as a proactive, self-healing biometric loop powered by three specialized adversarial agents:
1. Bio-Optimizer Agent: Extracts calories, macros, micronutrients, and glycemic load from meal photos, dynamically recalculating today's diet and workout volume.
2. Physiological Stress-Tester Agent: Actively red-teams the proposed protocol against overtraining, nutrient deficiencies, and metabolic plateaus.
3. Clinical & Safety Judge Agent: Enforces strict biomechanical guardrails so injured joints/muscles are never overloaded, committing permanent adaptations to a local bio_learnings.md ledger.

Key Features

• Multimodal Meal Vision: Snap a photo of any meal to immediately extract macro/micronutrient data and auto-adjust your daily intake.
• Always-On 5×1 cm Quick-Bar & Cross-Device Sync: Instant voice dictation, photo capture (+), and Ctrl + Space desktop overlay with Picture-in-Picture float—seamlessly synced across desktop and mobile.
• Persistent bio_learnings.md Memory: Your system remembers food sensitivities, strength progression, and rehab recovery milestones forever.
• Zero-Knowledge Federated Learning: Anonymized recovery patterns strengthen the global clinical engine without personal health data ever leaving your environment.
